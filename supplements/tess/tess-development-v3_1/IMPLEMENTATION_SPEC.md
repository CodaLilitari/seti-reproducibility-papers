# TESS development v3.1: offline photometry, timing and freeze contract

Coda, collaborating with Andrei and Lilitari. 2026-10-08. This specifies development-only code and synthetic tests. It is not an activated unfamiliar-host survey, calibrated astronomical uncertainty model, live metadata client, or claim of complete physical vetting. The original prototype and prior packets remain unchanged. The metadata envelope/release contract is specified separately in `METADATA_COMPLETENESS_SPEC.md`.

## Inputs and implemented boundary

The raw-array preprocessing component implements common QUALITY==0 support with finite positive SAP/PDC flux and formal errors, sector-by-sector median normalization, a one-day running median split at gaps strictly greater than 0.5 day, and propagation of formal errors through the same normalization/trend. The running window includes both endpoints; no transit-depth clipping occurs. Missing/rejected raw samples remain visible in its common-mask ledger. Separate sectors never share a median window. Real FITS transport, header/time-system verification and extraction remain outer adapters; the component never imports real FITS or calls a network.

The subsequent photometric API accepts strictly increasing finite preprocessed times, relative flux residuals, positive detrended formal errors, an explicit nominal cadence clock and assigned time-span endpoints. The connected synthetic driver starts with raw SAP/PDC/error/quality/sector arrays, calls the preprocessing component, and exercises discovery, timing, freeze and holdout. Synthetic success does not certify the missing outer I/O adapters. Holdout span, timestamps and clock are validated before classification; the holdout span must start strictly after the entire discovery span.

The default search uses 0.6–13 days, durations 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4 hours, Astropy BLS likelihood/fast/oversample 10, and three attempt slots. Its objective is separate from the local shoulder-depth screen. Errors are supplied formal errors; no empirical FAP or completeness calculation is implemented.

## Grid and bounded resources

Let B=max(t)-min(t), including any gap between discovery sectors. The largest frequency step is `(1/24)/(5*B*13)` for the default duration/range. Set Ninterval=ceil((1/Pmin-1/Pmax)/step_max) and use equally spaced frequencies including both endpoints. Store the complete period grid and its hash. The bound concerns period-grid phase drift over B only; it is never used as an ephemeris error bar.

Fixed-size chunks evaluate the same stored grid, not independent adaptive grids. Choose the global maximum by descending power, then ascending period, duration, epoch and grid index. No coarsening, thinning or additional candidate opportunity occurs at chunk boundaries. Default chunks hold 512 trial periods. The explicit default development resource ceiling is 250,000 grid points; larger requests raise `ResourceStop`, rather than return a coarser result. A 730-day planning example exceeds that limit. Raising the limit is a separately frozen resource choice, not an automatic fallback.

## Attempt and mask ledger

All three attempt slots are recorded before any holdout evaluation. A computationally valid maximum supplies its original period/epoch/duration mask even if screening later fails, including low score or inadequate event coverage. A failed computation without a finite maximum supplies no mask and retains its failure record. No failed maximum is replaced by a fourth. Sequential search uses masks from earlier computed maxima.

After all three slots, the final ledger includes every computed maximum. Each candidate excludes its own mask and applies all other masks, including those from later or screen-failed maxima. Every mask uses the original search ephemeris and half-width 0.75*duration. The same unaltered other-mask ledger is extrapolated into holdout; mask ephemerides are never refined based on holdout. Lost support is recorded for every event window. Original-search masks are a declared approximation whose long-baseline timing error is not calibrated.

## Coverage and event depth

For a predicted midpoint T and duration D, use half-open windows `[T-1.5D,T-.5D)`, `[T-.5D,T+.5D)`, `[T+.5D,T+1.5D)`. The nominal lattice supplied by `CadenceClock` defines the expected count, including rejected/missing/out-of-sector cadences. Every window needs at least 70% of expected cadences and at least five unique accepted cadence IDs. Other-signal masks count as missing coverage. Duplicate mapped cadence IDs prevent that event from qualifying, including duplicate IDs across the union of all three windows. The outer real-FITS cadence-origin/CADENCENO-to-barycentric-time contract is not implemented; it must establish that this nominal clock is appropriate before application to a real product.

Record every event center in the caller's assigned discovery/holdout span, including uncovered ones. Discovery spans include intervening gaps. For each covered event, depth is inverse-variance weighted shoulder mean minus inverse-variance weighted inside mean. Variance is `1/sum(w_out)+1/sum(w_in)`. Combine **all** covered event depths by inverse variance, with no individual sign veto. Insufficient covered events are inconclusive, not a negative planet finding.

For each successfully computed combination, v3.1 adds `event_contributions`: one row for every covered event in original input order, with its zero-based `event_index`, `normalized_inverse_variance_weight` `a_i=w_i/sum(w)`, and signed `signed_snr_contribution` `c_i=w_i*d_i/sqrt(sum(w))`, where `w_i=1/variance_i`. Thus the weights sum to one and the signed contributions sum to the existing formal combined S/N, within floating-point arithmetic. Negative and zero contributions remain in the list. Uncovered events remain in the event ledger but do not enter the existing aggregate or these diagnostics. Insufficient or invalid combinations retain their original inconclusive result without fabricated weights.

The aggregate field `max_absolute_snr_contribution_fraction` is `max(abs(c_i))/sum(abs(c_i))`. If that denominator is zero, the fraction is JSON `null` and `contribution_concentration_unavailable_reason` is `zero_total_absolute_snr_contribution`; otherwise the reason is `null`. Opposite-sign cancellation alone does not make this denominator zero. These are reporting diagnostics only: no veto, threshold, event selection, mask, timing, freeze policy or recovery criterion changes. Equal-variance examples `[-0.1]+[2.2]*9` and `[-0.1,19.72]+[0.01]*8` have the same aggregate depth 1.97 and formal S/N about 6.22969, but concentration fractions about 0.110553 and 0.990955. Both retain the same recovery classification when discovery depth is consistent.

The original maximum's coverage/depth screen is retained as a diagnostic. After discovery-only ephemeris fitting, remeasure discovery coverage and depth once at that fitted candidate ephemeris, with unchanged duration, the same other-mask ledger, and the same estimator used for holdout. This final fitted-ephemeris depth is the depth compared to holdout. If the set of adequately covered event cycles changes after fitting, candidate timing becomes inconclusive; no favorable subset or repeated fit/reassessment loop is used. Changes in sample membership/counts within the same covered event set are recorded. The final discovery screen needs at least three covered events, positive aggregate depth and formal combined S/N>=8.

## Discovery-only timing and formal covariance

Each adequately covered original discovery event is fitted over its entire three-window region after other masks. Fix a trapezoid with total duration D and ingress/egress each 0.2D. Fit four parameters simultaneously: local baseline, local slope scaled by D, **signed** depth, and center shift in `[-D/2,+D/2]`. The analytic weighted Jacobian includes all four parameters. No local event-depth sign is an eligibility requirement. Optimize weighted least squares, at most 200 evaluations, with fixed tolerances in the code.

Require an interior center (at least 1e-4*D away from the shift bounds), at least two samples in each fitted ingress/egress, finite values, and full-rank information. For conditioning, normalize Jacobian columns before checking the four-by-four information matrix; reject condition number above 1e12. Transform the full inverse information back to parameter units. Center variance includes nuisance-parameter covariance. Formal errors are not multiplied by reduced chi square; that statistic is recorded as a model-mismatch diagnostic only. Fixed-shape mismatch, red noise, detrending uncertainty and dilution are not calibrated by this covariance.

Every adequately covered original event must return a valid timing fit. If any fails, the candidate timing is inconclusive; do not select only favorable event times. At least three unique, exact-integer event cycles are required. Fit an inverse-variance weighted straight line through all those centers, with its two-parameter analytic covariance. Epoch is referenced to the earliest included cycle; covariance is stored in epoch/period units. Duplicate, ambiguous or malformed cycles, singular information, nonpositive periods, or unsupported covariance are inconclusive.

Alias resolution is an explicit discovery-only caller input. `unresolved` or undeclared aliases force timing inconclusive; this implementation does not determine the true orbital period or prove alias uniqueness. The synthetic driver can state resolution because the generating model is known. A real alias-adjudication policy remains an outer gate.

## Freeze and holdout

Serialize all three attempts, failures, original and fitted discovery diagnostics, timing parameters, full event list, final ledger, grid, code/runtime/dependency identity, input-array identity and configuration together. Commit the assigned holdout span and cadence clock in this discovery freeze, before holdout data; neither may be narrowed or changed during evaluation. Hash the canonical JSON. Holdout evaluation verifies the hash, executing source identity and exact Python/NumPy/SciPy/Astropy identities before calculations. The freeze API accepts holdout assignment metadata but has no holdout flux input. The archive/storage and access separation enforcing that no human or unrelated program inspected holdout data is an outer operational gate, not something a local hash proves.

Propagate timing variance as `C00+2*n*C01+n*n*C11`. Any unestimable/invalid variance or 3sigma exceeding D/2 at any evaluated holdout event makes the outcome inconclusive. No period, phase, duration, mask or uncertainty refit is permitted on holdout.

Recovery requires at least two covered predicted holdout events; combine **all** covered events, including negatives and zeros. Require positive aggregate depth, formal aggregate S/N>=5, and absolute discovery/holdout depth difference <=3 times their combined standard error. There is **no every-event-positive gate**. Covered failures are non-recoveries. Example equal-sigma event scores `[-0.1,2.2,2.2,2.2,2.2,2.2,2.2,2.2,2.2,2.2]` have combined S/N approximately 6.22969 and can pass when discovery depth is consistent. `Phi(1)^25` is approximately 0.0133153, illustrating the strong selection introduced by the removed sign rule.

## Explicit remaining gates

No live catalog/product transport or source-specific pagination adapter; no unfamiliar-star acquisition; no real FITS header/extraction/cadence-clock adapter; no empirical correlated-noise challenge calibration; no data-driven alias adjudicator; no physical transit model/dilution uncertainty; no SAP comparison orchestrator; no pixel localization, EB/secondary/neighbor vetting, novelty audit, publication or operational access control. Array-level quality/normalization/detrending is implemented, but source-level preprocessing validation remains external. Metadata and photometric components are reusable offline development pieces. The synthetic connected driver demonstrates their implemented photometric path without claiming these outer stages complete.

The synthetic example uses a declared 2–4 day range and one 2-hour duration to bound the fixture; it is not an adopted revision of the proposed survey choices. Its original sector-edge variant is retained as an inconclusive regression because the covered-event set changes after ephemeris fitting. A separate fully covered fixture exercises recovery. No threshold is altered to rescue the edge case.
